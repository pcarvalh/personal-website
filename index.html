I work at the intersection of learning science and educational technology, leveraging AI to transform educational experiences through practice-oriented learning environments. My research focuses on three areas: using data analytics and computational modeling to understand student behavior; developing predictive models that enhance learning outcomes; and exploring how generative AI can strengthen practice-based learning while enabling teachers to offer more individualized guidance.
